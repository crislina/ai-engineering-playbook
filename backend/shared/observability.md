# Observability

## Purpose

Help teams understand runtime behavior through logs, metrics, traces, and alerts.

## Principles

- Preserve correlation or trace IDs across service boundaries.
- Emit metrics for critical workflows, dependency calls, and failure rates.
- Make failed background work observable and recoverable.
- Alert on user-impacting failures, not only infrastructure symptoms.

## Signals

- Logs explain what happened.
- Metrics show how often and how much.
- Traces show where time was spent.
- Alerts tell humans when action is needed.
